How To Battle

Both you and the computer select a weapon simultaneously: Rock, Paper, or Scissors. Each choice triumphs over another according to ancient combat logic:

VICTORY
Rock crushes Scissors

Dense solid stone blunts and breaks steel blades.

VICTORY
Scissors cuts Paper

Twin sharpened edges slice paper clean into pieces.

VICTORY
Paper covers Rock

Broad flexible sheet envelops and neutralizes the boulder.

STALEMATE
Same Pick = Draw

When both warriors summon identical weapons, neither gains ground.
